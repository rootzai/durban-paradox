# Future Concept: The French 75 Integration Plan

## Thematic Resonance
The French 75 represents the perfect intersection of the Glassworthy themes:
1.  **Precision & Power:** Just as the "Eighth" is a unit of precision, the "75" is a unit of artillery precision.
2.  **Elegance & Danger:** The cocktail (champagne, sugar) masks the weapon (gin, artillery), mirroring the "pudding" trap Malcolm warns about—comfort hiding danger.
3.  **Historical Irony:** The shift from "modern marvel" to "industrial slaughter" parallels the Glassworthy family's struggle with their own inventions being misused by Kings.

## Narrative Integration Points

### Chapter 4: The Tetrad (Foundation) - *Foreshadowing*
*   **Scene:** Malcolm, now older, visits a World's Fair-style exhibition in Paris (late 1890s timeline shift or vision).
*   **Event:** He witnesses the unveiling of the *Canon de 75*. The crowd applauds its "elegant recoil mechanism."
*   **Dialogue:** Malcolm remarks, "They applaud the math, unaware that the equation ends in silence." He compares the recoil to a "kick of gin."

### Chapter 7: The Indian Impossibility - *The Cocktail's Birth*
*   **Scene:** A bar in Paris, 1915. Soldiers on leave.
*   **Action:** A bartender mixes the drink for a weary officer. "Light as a feather, hits like a cannon," he jokes.
*   **Glassworthy Connection:** The "gin" used is revealed to be distilled with a specific botanical from the Glassworthy herb garden (the *Special Tea* variant), linking the drink's "kick" to the family's psychoactive history.

### Chapter 9: The IX Departure - *The Dark Irony*
*   **Scene:** Post-WWI. The "Lost Generation" drinking in a jazz cellar.
*   **Monologue:** A character toasts with a French 75. "To the gun that saved us, and the drink that helps us forget it."
*   **The Paradox:** The drink becomes a symbol of the "Durban Paradox"—consuming the very thing that destroyed you to feel alive.

## Artifact Ideas
1.  **The 75mm Shell Casing:** A brass shell casing that has been converted into a cocktail shaker.
2.  **The Recipe Card:** A stained index card with the recipe, but the ingredients are listed as chemical propellants (Cordite, Nitro, etc.) instead of Gin and Lemon.
3.  **The Recoil Diagram:** A technical schematic of the gun's hydro-pneumatic recoil system, overlaid with the fluid dynamics of a champagne bubble rising.

## "Super Mathematics" Connection
*   **7 + 5 = 12:** The number 12 (The Dozen) is a "perfect" merchant number (divisible by 1, 2, 3, 4, 6).
*   **75mm:** The caliber is a metric measurement, clashing with the "Imperial Eighth." This represents the clash between the old Glassworthy world (Imperial, mystical) and the new modern world (Metric, industrial).
*   **The "Kick":** The recoil of the gun allows it to stay in place (stability through force). The drink provides stability (numbness) through intoxication. A paradox of motion.
