**Design Movement**: Renaissance Revival / Digital Parchment
**Core Principles**:
1.  **Historical Immersion**: The user should feel like they are reading a manuscript from 1499, but with modern interactivity.
2.  **Mathematical Elegance**: The design should reflect the beauty of geometry and the precision of MPG's mind.
3.  **Cannabis Mysticism**: Subtle, organic elements that hint at the "green" influence without being overt or tacky.
4.  **Narrative Flow**: The design should guide the reader through the story, with each chapter feeling like a distinct step in the journey.

**Color Philosophy**:
*   **Primary**: Aged Parchment (#F5E6D3) - To evoke the feeling of old paper and history.
*   **Secondary**: Deep Burgundy (#8B2635) - Represents the richness of the Renaissance and the passion of the characters.
*   **Accent**: Golden Amber (#D4A574) - For highlights, navigation, and important elements, symbolizing wealth and enlightenment.
*   **Text**: Dark Sepia (#3E2723) - Softer than black, easier on the eyes, and fits the historical theme.
*   **Cannabis Element**: Sage Green (#556B2F) - A muted, natural green for subtle accents and botanical motifs.

**Layout Paradigm**:
*   **Book-Like Structure**: The main content area should feel like an open book or a scroll.
*   **Sidebar Navigation**: A vertical navigation bar on the left (or right) that resembles a bookmark or a table of contents.
*   **Asymmetric Balance**: Avoid perfect symmetry; use the "Golden Ratio" to place elements in a way that feels natural and pleasing to the eye.

**Signature Elements**:
*   **Drop Caps**: Large, ornate initial letters for the start of each chapter.
*   **Marginalia**: Notes, diagrams, and "handwritten" comments in the margins (using tooltips or side panels).
*   **Geometric Overlays**: Subtle, faint geometric patterns (circles, triangles, spirals) in the background to represent the mathematical themes.

**Interaction Philosophy**:
*   **Slow Reveal**: Content should fade in gently as the user scrolls, mimicking the act of reading and discovery.
*   **Tactile Feedback**: Buttons and links should have a subtle "press" effect or a color shift that feels responsive and grounded.
*   **Hover States**: Hovering over character names or key terms could reveal a small portrait or definition.

**Animation**:
*   **Page Turn Effect**: Smooth transitions between chapters that mimic the turning of a page.
*   **Ink Bleed**: Loading animations that look like ink spreading on paper.
*   **Floating Geometry**: Subtle, slow-moving geometric shapes in the background to add depth and life.

**Typography System**:
*   **Headings**: *Cinzel* or *Cormorant Garamond* - Elegant, serif fonts with a historical feel.
*   **Body**: *Crimson Text* or *EB Garamond* - Highly readable serif fonts that look good on screens but retain a classic aesthetic.
*   **Accents**: *Pinyon Script* - For signatures, marginalia, and special emphasis.

**Design Movement**: Celestial Navigation / Star Chart Aesthetic
**Core Principles**:
1.  **Cosmic Connection**: Emphasize the connection between the sea, the stars, and mathematics.
2.  **Dark Mode Default**: A deep, dark background to represent the night sky and the mysteries of the universe.
3.  **Constellation Connectivity**: Visual lines connecting ideas, characters, and chapters like stars in a constellation.
4.  **Precision & Clarity**: Despite the dark theme, the content must be crisp, clear, and easy to read.

**Color Philosophy**:
*   **Primary**: Midnight Blue (#0B1026) - The color of the deep ocean and the night sky.
*   **Secondary**: Starlight Silver (#E0E0E0) - For text and primary lines.
*   **Accent**: Navigational Gold (#FFD700) - For key interactive elements and "current location" indicators.
*   **Highlight**: Nebula Purple (#6A0DAD) - For magical/cannabis elements, representing the "higher state of consciousness."

**Layout Paradigm**:
*   **Central Axis**: A central line (the "course") that runs through the page, with content branching off it.
*   **Radial Menus**: Navigation that expands outward from a central point, like a compass rose.
*   **Floating Cards**: Content contained in semi-transparent cards that float above the starfield background.

**Signature Elements**:
*   **Astrolabe Motifs**: Circular, mechanical design elements inspired by navigational instruments.
*   **Connecting Lines**: Thin lines that draw themselves between related concepts as the user scrolls.
*   **Star Particles**: A subtle, interactive background of stars that move slightly with the mouse.

**Interaction Philosophy**:
*   **Exploration**: The user feels like they are navigating a ship or a telescope, discovering content as they go.
*   **Zoom & Pan**: Transitions that zoom into a specific "star" (chapter) or pan across the "sky" (timeline).

**Animation**:
*   **Orbiting Elements**: Small icons or indicators that orbit around larger focal points.
*   **Twinkle**: Subtle pulsing of key elements to draw attention.
*   **Constellation Draw**: Lines connecting stars to form shapes when a chapter is selected.

**Typography System**:
*   **Headings**: *Playfair Display* - High contrast, elegant, and slightly modern but with classical roots.
*   **Body**: *Lato* or *Montserrat* - Clean, geometric sans-serif to contrast with the ornate headings and ensure readability against the dark background.
*   **Data/Numbers**: *Space Mono* - For coordinates, dates, and mathematical formulas.

**Design Movement**: Botanical Mathematics / Fibonacci Nature
**Core Principles**:
1.  **Organic Geometry**: Showcasing the mathematics found in nature (spirals, fractals, symmetry).
2.  **Growth & Evolution**: The design should feel like it is growing and evolving as the user progresses.
3.  **Cannabis as Nature**: Presenting cannabis as a beautiful, natural plant with mathematical structure.
4.  **Light & Air**: A bright, airy aesthetic that feels fresh and enlightening.

**Color Philosophy**:
*   **Primary**: Crisp White (#FFFFFF) - For a clean, modern, and academic feel.
*   **Secondary**: Leaf Green (#4CAF50) - Vibrant and alive, representing the plant and growth.
*   **Accent**: Earthy Brown (#795548) - For structure and grounding elements.
*   **Highlight**: Fractal Blue (#2196F3) - For mathematical concepts and diagrams, contrasting with the organic green.

**Layout Paradigm**:
*   **Golden Spiral**: Layouts that follow the Fibonacci spiral, guiding the eye naturally through the content.
*   **Grid Breaking**: Elements that break the grid in organic ways, like a vine growing over a trellis.
*   **Split Screen**: One side for text, the other for dynamic, generative visualizations of the concepts.

**Signature Elements**:
*   **Fractal Patterns**: Backgrounds and dividers generated by fractal algorithms.
*   **Botanical Illustrations**: Detailed, scientific drawings of cannabis and other plants, overlaid with geometric lines.
*   **Data Visualization**: Interactive charts and graphs that visualize the "Influence Value" and other metrics.

**Interaction Philosophy**:
*   **Growth**: Elements "grow" onto the screen (scale up, unfold) rather than just fading in.
*   **Manipulation**: Users can interact with the mathematical models (e.g., adjust a variable in the game theory simulation).

**Animation**:
*   **Unfolding**: Menus and sections unfold like leaves or petals.
*   **Flow**: Smooth, fluid movements that mimic water or wind.
*   **Morphing**: Shapes that morph from organic to geometric and back.

**Typography System**:
*   **Headings**: *Philosopher* - A font with curved, organic lines that mimic nature.
*   **Body**: *Merriweather* - A highly readable serif that feels comfortable and grounded.
*   **Captions**: *Fira Code* - A monospaced font for code snippets or mathematical formulas.
